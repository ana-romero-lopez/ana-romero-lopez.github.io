01
Competitive intelligence · Higher education
Role: Research design, fieldwork, data analysis and strategy

Mystery shopping of 37 competitors to size our bet on the francophone market

Context

An analysis of the francophone higher-education market to gauge how strong our bet on it could be in the 2027 forecast.

What I did

  • Study design and data model: 37 institutions across 7 programme areas, mapped by country, programme and competitor type, in a single data model covering the contact log, landing pages, social media, Meta Ad Library, Google results, ChatGPT visibility, brochures and prices normalised to euros.
  • Clean, automated tracking: every touchpoint categorised by channel, sender, automated or human, content and next step, with templated follow-ups and responses to keep the fictitious prospect profile consistent and the dataset clean.
  • Sales-process stress test: mapping each competitor's friction points and objection handling — speed to lead, qualification questions, sales arguments, offers, urgency tactics and follow-up cadence.
  • Full-funnel audit: SEO with geolocated Google searches, AI-search visibility in ChatGPT, paid social through 94 active Meta ads, landing pages (mobile PageSpeed, messaging, forms), organic social and Google Maps.
  • Analysis and score modelling: pattern identification across the best performers and a weighted score model to position our offer against the market, delivered as an analysis report with prioritised recommendations for management.

Competitor names, prices and our own results are not disclosed.

02
Full-funnel analytics · Marketing ops · Higher education
Role: Designed and built it

A full-funnel dashboard connecting marketing, sales and CRM
by programme, intake and country

Challenge

In a mid-sized organisation where departments have to work closely together, separate reports only add friction: to selling the product and to keeping teams aligned. The decision was to unify everything in one dashboard, so each team sees its own part and understands the other perspectives that shape the product funnel — Meta leads by region, SEO and SEM data from our external agencies, opportunities in Atenea (the group's CRM on Microsoft Dynamics 365) and enrolment targets per intake.

We also added a data-veracity filter, to be sure decisions rested on real data and not on a hypothetical bubble of what the product was delivering.

What it covers · 11 views in 4 areas

  • Strategy: overview and a marketing-to-sales view with lead-to-enrolment conversion by campaign, programme, intake and country. Each campaign is classified automatically as Scale · Converts despite friction · Volume without quality · Review.
  • Digital marketing: lead quality by campaign, country and programme, weekly lead evolution, and SEO and SEM KPIs from our external agencies, by objective and geographic focus.
  • Sales: every Atenea opportunity followed through the commercial funnel — appointment, interview, documentation, admissions committee, proposal and payment — with time to first contact, contact rate, loss reasons and enrolments against target for each intake.
  • Data veracity: every upload is validated before it reaches reporting — country-code mapping, campaign names consistent between Meta and the CRM, out-of-range values and reporting-period alignment. Exceptions are flagged for review and source data is never overwritten.

How it is built

A single-file web app (HTML, JavaScript, SheetJS, Chart.js) that reads Excel exports directly, with no server or licence. It works with any Excel file whose columns follow the defined data schema, applies the team's reporting rules — commercial weeks, campaign-name mapping across file versions, pacing against each intake's start date — and is refreshed with every CRM and Meta export.

Designing a testimonial and alumni strategy from scratch

Challenge

Turning paid-media volume into enrolments needed structured follow-up at every stage: nurturing new leads, recovering those sales could not reach, re-engaging graduates and building local social proof. There was no solid structure in place for lead follow-up or for collecting alumni testimonials.

Testimonials system

A repeatable process designed from the ground up: a tracking sheet fed from the CRM, an email flow triggered three to four weeks after each intake starts, a short interview guide and video specifications. Each testimonial feeds a multichannel strategy in five formats:

Satisfaction surveys

Design, analysis and follow-up of satisfaction surveys to assess programme quality from an emotional angle as well as a data-driven one. Combining both perspectives shows where the real drivers of satisfaction are, and which stories are worth turning into testimonials.

06
Marketing automation · AI · Open source
Role: Designed and built it at Otomatico · Python, Meta Marketing API, Claude API

Meta Ads AI Monitor: daily campaign diagnosis with the Claude API

Challenge

Reviewing CPL, CTR, CPM and frequency campaign by campaign is time-consuming, and performance drops are often detected only after the budget has been spent. Turning the numbers into the right action also requires context.

What I built

  • Daily pipeline at 6:00: pulls every campaign from the Meta Marketing API and compares the last 7 days with the previous 7.
  • Threshold alerts on CTR, CPL, CPM and frequency, with CPL calculated on lead actions only.
  • AI differential diagnosis (Claude API): the prompt requires the model to cross at least two metrics and assign one of nine root causes, such as creative fatigue, audience saturation, delivery restriction or a post-click funnel issue.
  • Actionable output: for each campaign, a priority level, a specific action, its rationale and one common mistake to avoid.
  • HTML email report with colour-coded week-on-week changes.

What Drives Me

Beyond the campaign

Beyond campaign performance, I care about what digital media is built for and who it includes. The platforms we choose, the content we create and the audiences we target carry a responsibility to reflect the full diversity of human experience.

That is why I research neurodivergence and AI: how artificial intelligence can be used to design more inclusive digital environments rather than replicate neurotypical norms.

I want to contribute to a digital landscape where inclusion is part of the strategy from the start, not an afterthought.
